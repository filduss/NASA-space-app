# Shipshape

A crew check-in for long missions that says whether a bad day is likely the person or likely the ship.

## Language

**Guide**:
The crew's built-in reference that explains, in plain words, what each reading means, why it matters in space and what NASA says about it. It is passive: it explains, it never judges a person's own data.
_Avoid_: Guidebook, help, manual, FAQ

**Guide entry**:
One topic in the Guide, written in fixed parts: what we measure, how to check it, why it matters in space, NASA's limit or target, what you can do, and when to tell the medical officer now. The last two appear only when a NASA source names them.
_Avoid_: Article, card, page

**Reading**:
One thing the app records for a person or the cabin: a check-in answer, a wearable value, a reaction-test result or a cabin sensor value.
_Avoid_: Measurement, metric, data point

**Cabin factor**:
A property of the ship's air that can explain how a person feels: CO₂, temperature or humidity.
_Avoid_: Environment variable, ship metric

**NASA risk**:
One of NASA's Human System Risks, the named health and performance risks of spaceflight that the Human Research Program works to reduce.
_Avoid_: HRP risk, hazard (hazard is the cause, risk is the outcome)
